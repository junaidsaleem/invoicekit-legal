BillBook User Guide

1. Shuruaat

App pehli dafa kholna aur apna business set karna

  1. Step 1

    App kholein

    1. Play Store se BillBook install karke kholein.
    2. 1Neeche hara Next button dabayein.
    3. 2Upar English dabakar Urdu ya Arabic bhi chun sakte hain.

  2. Step 2

    App ke baare mein

    1. Ye screens batati hain ke app kya karti hai.
    2. 1Next dabate jayein.

  3. Step 3

    Aap ka data aap ke paas

    1. Koi account ya sign-up nahi. Sab kuch sirf aap ke phone mein rehta hai.
    2. 1Get Started dabayein.

  4. Step 4

    Apna business set karein

    1. 1Upload Logo dabakar apna logo lagayein (zaroori nahi).
    2. 2Business Name mein apni dukaan / company ka naam likhein.
    3. Ye maloomat har invoice par chhapegi.

  5. Step 5

    Number aur pata likhein

    1. 1Phone Number likhein, jaise 03001234567.
    2. 2Address mein dukaan ka pata likhein.
    3. NTN / STRN sirf tab likhein agar aap ke paas hain.

  6. Step 6

    Save karein

    1. 1Save & Start dabayein.
    2. Purana backup hai? Have a backup? Restore it dabayein.

  7. Step 7

    Dashboard (main screen)

    1. 1+ New Quotation se naya rate (quotation) banayein.
    2. 2+ New Invoice se naya bill banayein.
    3. Neeche tabs: Dashboard, Documents, Catalog, Clients, Settings.

2. Letterhead, Signature aur Stamp

Apna printed letterhead, dastakhat aur mohar ek dafa lagayein — har invoice aur quotation par khud aayenge

  1. Step 8

    Letterhead kholein

    1. Settings tab → Letterhead dabayein.
    2. 1Apne printed letterhead ki photo ho to Pick File. Abhi photo leni ho to Take Photo.
    3. Tip: letterhead ko seedha, achi roshni mein, poora page frame mein rakh kar photo lein.

  2. Step 9

    Photos ki ijazat dein

    1. Pehli dafa phone ijazat maangta hai.
    2. 1Allow Full Access (ya Allow) dabayein.

  3. Step 10

    Letterhead ki photo chunein

    1. 1Apne letterhead ki photo par dabayein.

  4. Step 11

    Check karein

    1. Letterhead poora aur seedha nazar aa raha ho.
    2. 1Theek hai to Use This. Nahi to Choose Another.

  5. Step 12

    Likhne ki jagah set karein

    1. 1Hara hissa woh jagah hai jahan invoice likha jayega. App ye khud dhoond leti hai.
    2. Header ya footer par likhai aa rahi ho to lakeerein ungli se khiskayein.
    3. 2Save Margins dabayein.

  6. Step 13

    Letterhead lag gaya

    1. Ab har invoice aur quotation isi letterhead par banega.
    2. 1Baad mein jagah theek karni ho to Adjust fit.
    3. 2Letterhead on all pages: band karein to sirf pehle page par aayega.

  7. Step 14

    Signature aur Stamp kholein

    1. Settings → Signature & Stamp kholein.
    2. 1Draw new: ungli se dastakhat karein.
    3. 2Signature ki photo hai to Upload image.
    4. 3Stamp (mohar) ke liye neeche wala Upload image.

  8. Step 15

    Dastakhat karein

    1. 1Safed dabbe mein ungli se dastakhat karein. Ghalti ho to Undo ya Clear All.
    2. Zyada jagah chahiye to phone ghuma lein (Rotate for more space).
    3. 2Save Signature dabayein.

  9. Step 16

    Stamp ki photo

    1. 1Upar Stamp chunein (pehle se Signature chuna ho sakta hai — zaroor check karein).
    2. 2Photo Library se mohar ki photo chunein, ya Take Photo.
    3. Tip: safed kaghaz par mohar laga kar seedhi photo lein.

  10. Step 17

    Stamp ki photo chunein

    1. 1Mohar wali photo par dabayein.

  11. Step 18

    Stamp check karke save karein

    1. 1Label mein naam likhein, jaise “Company Stamp”.
    2. 2App safed background khud hata deti hai. Dekhein ke letterhead par kaisa lagega.
    3. Rang theek na ho to Force black ink; terhi ho to Straighten.
    4. 3Save Stamp dabayein.

  12. Step 19

    Kahan lagana hai, chunein

    1. 1Ye teen buttons on karein:
    2. Show on invoices — har invoice par dastakhat.
    3. Show on quotations — har quotation par dastakhat.
    4. Show stamp — dastakhat ke saath mohar.

  13. Step 20

    Preview aur jagah

    1. 1Neeche preview mein dekhein ke stamp aur signature kaise lagenge.
    2. 2Daayein, baayein ya beech mein lagana ho to Adjust placement.

  14. Step 21

    Left, Centre ya Right

    1. 1Signature ki jagah chunein: Left, Centre ya Right.
    2. 2Save Placement dabayein.

  15. Step 22

    Har invoice par khud

    1. 1Naya invoice banate waqt step 2 mein Signature ✓ · Stamp ✓ nazar aayega.
    2. Kisi ek invoice par na lagana ho to is par dabayein.

  16. Step 23

    Sirf is invoice ke liye

    1. 1Include signature / Include stamp band ya on karein.
    2. Ye sirf isi invoice ke liye hai. Settings wali default nahi badalti.

  17. Step 24

    Invoice tayyar: letterhead + stamp + signature

    1. 1Upar aap ka letterhead.
    2. 2Neeche aap ki mohar aur dastakhat, “Authorised Signatory” ke saath.
    3. 3Footer bhi letterhead wala.
    4. “DRAFT” sirf preview mein hai — customer ko bheji gayi PDF mein nahi hota.

  18. Step 25

    Quotation par bhi

    1. 1Quotation bhi aap ke letterhead par banti hai.
    2. Validity aur payment terms khud likhe aate hain.

  19. Step 26

    Quotation ka aakhri page

    1. 1For Al-Madina Traders: aap ki mohar aur dastakhat.
    2. 2Accepted by client: customer yahan sign karke wapas bhej sakta hai.

  20. Step 27

    Printed letterhead paper par print

    1. Agar aap ke paas letterhead wala chhapa hua kaghaz hai:
    2. 1Preview mein Print on paper chunein — letterhead PDF se hat jayega, sirf likhai chhapegi.
    3. 2Print signature: mohar/dastakhat bhi chhapein. Leave blank: haath se sign ki jagah.
    4. WhatsApp par bhejne ke liye wapas Share as PDF chunein.

3. Apna Maal (Catalog)

Jo cheezein aap bechte hain, unhein ek dafa save karein

  1. Step 28

    Catalog kholein

    1. Neeche Catalog tab dabayein.
    2. 1Add Item dabayein.
    3. Excel / CSV file se bohat saari cheezein ek saath: Import from file.

  2. Step 29

    Cheez ka naam likhein

    1. 1Item Name mein cheez ka naam likhein, jaise “Cement Bag (50kg)”.

  3. Step 30

    Rate aur tax

    1. 1Rate mein ek cheez ki qeemat likhein.
    2. 2GST % sirf tab likhein agar aap tax lagate hain (jaise 17).
    3. 3Save Item dabayein.

  4. Step 31

    Aap ki list tayyar

    1. 1Saari cheezein yahan nazar aayengi.
    2. Kisi cheez par dabakar rate badal sakte hain.
    3. ⋮ dabakar delete bhi kar sakte hain.

4. Customers (Clients)

Apne customers ka naam aur number save karein

  1. Step 32

    Clients kholein

    1. 1Neeche Clients tab dabayein, phir Add Client.
    2. 2Phone ke contacts se lena ho to Select many from Contacts.

  2. Step 33

    Customer ki maloomat

    1. 1Naam, phone number aur pata likhein. Urdu naam bhi chalta hai.
    2. 2Save Client dabayein.

  3. Step 34

    Customer save ho gaya

    1. 1Customer list mein aa gaya.
    2. Us par dabakar maloomat badal sakte hain.

5. Invoice Banana

Bill banayein aur WhatsApp par bhejein

  1. Step 35

    Naya invoice shuru karein

    1. Dashboard par + New Invoice dabayein.
    2. 1Select Client dabayein.
    3. Invoice number aur tareekh app khud likh deti hai.

  2. Step 36

    Customer chunein

    1. 1List mein se apna customer dabayein.
    2. Naya customer? Add New Client dabayein.

  3. Step 37

    Cheezein add karein

    1. 1+ Add Item dabayein.

  4. Step 38

    Catalog se cheez chunein

    1. 1Har cheez ke saamne + dabayein.
    2. 2Jo cheez catalog mein nahi, us ke liye Enter one-off item.
    3. Aakhir mein Done dabayein.

  5. Step 39

    Tadaad (quantity) badlein

    1. 1+ aur − se tadaad badlein. 🗑 se cheez hata dein.
    2. 2Sab theek ho to Continue dabayein.
    3. Cheez ke naam par dabakar rate ya tadaad type bhi kar sakte hain.

  6. Step 40

    Tadaad type karna

    1. 1Quantity mein tadaad likhein (jaise 50 kg).
    2. 2Save Changes dabayein.

  7. Step 41

    Total aur discount

    1. 1Discount mein riyayat (rupay) likhein.
    2. 2Grand Total app khud nikaal deti hai, GST ke saath.
    3. Tax nahi lagana? Charge GST wala button band kar dein.

  8. Step 42

    Due date aur notes

    1. 1Due Date: paise kab tak milne chahiyein.
    2. 2Signature & Stamp se apna dastakhat lagayein.
    3. 3Preview Invoice dabayein.

  9. Step 43

    Invoice dekhein aur bhejein

    1. 1Send on WhatsApp dabayein.
    2. 2Sirf PDF save karni ho to Save PDF.
    3. Invoice bilkul aise hi customer ko jayega.

  10. Step 44

    WhatsApp chunein

    1. 1Yahan WhatsApp ka icon dabayein, phir customer chunein aur bhej dein.
    2. Email ya print ke liye bhi yahi jagah hai.

  11. Step 45

    Kya bhej diya?

    1. 1Agar customer ko bhej diya hai to Yes, it is sent dabayein.
    2. Is ke baad invoice ke number aur rate lock ho jate hain.

  12. Step 46

    Invoice bhej diya!

    1. Mubarak ho! Aap ka pehla invoice tayyar hai.
    2. 1View Documents se saare invoice dekhein.
    3. Duplicate se isi jaisa naya invoice jaldi banayein.

6. Payment Likhna

Customer ne paise diye to app mein darj karein

  1. Step 47

    Invoice kholein

    1. 1Documents tab mein invoice par dabayein.

  2. Step 48

    Payment darj karein

    1. Balance remaining batata hai kitne paise baaqi hain.
    2. 1Record Payment dabayein.

  3. Step 49

    Kitne paise mile

    1. 1Amount mein mili hui raqam likhein.
    2. 2Kaise mile: Cash, Bank, Easypaisa ya Other.
    3. 3Save Payment dabayein.

  4. Step 50

    Baaqi raqam

    1. 1Ab baaqi raqam khud kam ho gayi.
    2. Invoice par bhi “Balance due” chhap jata hai.

  5. Step 51

    Kaun kitna dene wala hai

    1. 1List mein har invoice ka haal: Partially Paid, Paid, Overdue.
    2. Upar Unpaid / Overdue dabakar sirf baaqi wale dekhein.

7. Quotation

Pehle rate batayein, phir usay invoice bana dein

  1. Step 52

    Quotation banayein

    1. 1Dashboard par + New Quotation dabayein (ya upar Quotation chunein).
    2. Customer aur cheezein bilkul invoice ki tarah add karein.

  2. Step 53

    Quotation bhejein

    1. 1Preview dekhein, phir Send on WhatsApp dabayein.
    2. Quotation par validity aur payment terms khud likhe aate hain.

  3. Step 54

    Quotation ko invoice banayein

    1. 1Customer maan jaye to quotation kholein, neeche scroll karein, Convert to Invoice dabayein.
    2. 2Sirf haan/na darj karni ho to Mark accepted / Mark rejected.

  4. Step 55

    Invoice tayyar

    1. 1Naya invoice ban gaya — cheezein dobara likhne ki zaroorat nahi.
    2. Ab isay bhi WhatsApp par bhej dein.

8. Settings

Design, backup, lock aur zubaan

  1. Step 56

    Settings

    1. 1Business Profile: naam, logo, bank details badlein.
    2. 2PDF Template: invoice ka design chunein.
    3. Letterhead aur Signature & Stamp bhi yahin se badal sakte hain (Section 2 dekhein).

  2. Step 57

    Invoice ka design

    1. 1Teen design: Classic Green, Modern Slate, Minimal White.
    2. Jis par dabayein wahi har invoice par lagega.

  3. Step 58

    Backup — bohat zaroori

    1. 1Backup & Restore: phone kho jaye to bhi data wapas mil jaye.
    2. 2App Lock: PIN ya fingerprint se app band karein.
    3. Language: English, اردو, العربية.

  4. Step 59

    Backup set karein

    1. 1Set Up Backup dabayein.
    2. 2Pehle ek backup password banana hoga.
    3. Backup Google Drive par bhi ja sakta hai.

  5. Step 60

    Backup password

    1. 1Kam az kam 10 harf ka password do dafa likhein.
    2. Password likh kar sambhal lein — bhool gaye to backup kabhi nahi khulega.
    3. 2Box par tick karein, phir Save Password.

  6. Step 61

    App Lock

    1. 1Ye button on karein aur 4–6 number ka PIN banayein.
    2. Fingerprint / Face ID se bhi khul sakti hai.

  7. Step 62

    Zubaan badlein

    1. 1English, اردو ya العربية par dabayein.
    2. App band ho kar nayi zubaan mein khulegi. Data mehfooz rehta hai.

Aksar pooche jane wale sawal

Phone badal liya, purana data kaise wapas aaye?

Purane phone par Settings → Backup & Restore se backup banayein. Naye phone par BillBook install karke setup screen par Have a backup? Restore it dabayein aur backup password likhein.

Backup password bhool gaya to?

Wo backup phir nahi khul sakta, kisi tareeqe se bhi nahi. Is liye password kahin likh kar mehfooz rakhein.

WhatsApp list mein nazar nahi aa raha

Share wali list mein daayein taraf More dabayein aur WhatsApp chunein. Phone mein WhatsApp install hona chahiye.

Kya internet zaroori hai?

Nahi. Invoice, quotation aur PDF sab internet ke baghair bante hain. Sirf WhatsApp par bhejne aur Google Drive backup ke liye internet chahiye.
