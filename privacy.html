Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Applies to BillBook v1.0.1 and later

BillBook ("the App") is an invoicing and quotation app for small businesses, published by Junaid Saleem ("we", "us"). It is built to be local-first on purpose: your business data is yours, and it stays on your device. This policy explains, plainly and specifically, what the App stores, what it sends and to whom, and why it asks for the device permissions it does.

1. The short version

BillBook has no account system and no server of ours. We never receive your business data. Nothing you enter leaves your device unless you choose to send it: by sharing a PDF or a backup file, or by turning on Google Drive backup, which stores an encrypted backup in your own Google Drive. The App contains no analytics or advertising. It does send anonymous crash reports (what went wrong, never your data or who you are) so problems can be fixed; you can turn them off in Settings. Reminders are made on your phone and nothing is sent to make them.

2. What the App stores, and where

Everything below is stored in an encrypted database on your device only. The encryption key is kept in your phone's secure key storage (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain).

Business profile — your business name, contact details, tax identifiers (such as GST/NTN/STRN, if you enter them), and logo/letterhead images.
Client records — names, phone numbers, and addresses you add or import from your device contacts.
Catalog — the items or services you sell, with prices and tax rates.
Documents — the invoices and quotations you create, including line items, totals, and payment records you log.
Signatures and stamps — images you draw, capture or upload to place on documents.
Error log — a short record of unexpected app errors (the error type, where it happened and technical details), kept so the App can be fixed. It never includes the error message text, client names, amounts or file contents, and it stays on your device unless you choose to send it from Settings → Share error report.

3. What leaves your device, and only when

Sharing documents. When you tap "Share" or "Send" on an invoice or quotation, the App hands the PDF to your device's share sheet, for example to WhatsApp or email. That is your explicit, one-time action; we have no visibility into what happens after you hand the file to another app.

Backups. A backup is a single file encrypted with a password you choose. We never see the password and cannot recover it. You can save or share the file yourself. If you turn on Google Drive backup, you sign in with Google and the App uploads encrypted backups to a "BillBook Backups" folder in your own Google Drive. The App asks only for permission to see files it created itself (the drive.file scope), not the rest of your Drive. Your Google account email is stored on your device to show which account is connected. Google's handling of your Drive is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. You can disconnect Drive in the App at any time.

Update check. The App downloads a small public file from GitHub Pages (junaidsaleem.github.io) to learn whether a newer version is available or required. The request contains no personal or business data. Like any web request, it reveals your device's IP address to GitHub, whose handling is covered by GitHub's privacy statement; we do not receive or log it.

Error reports. If you choose Share error report in Settings, the error log described above is shared through your device's share sheet to whoever you send it to.

Crash reports. When the App crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends an anonymous report to BugSnag, a crash-reporting service run by SmartBear Software, so we can find and fix the problem. It also sends how long the App took to start. Crash reports are on by default; you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Crash reports, and from then on nothing more is sent.

A crash report contains only: the type of error, the places in the App's own code where it happened (file names and line numbers), the App version, and your phone's model and operating-system version. Before a report leaves your phone, the App removes everything else. A crash report never contains your name, your business details, client names, phone numbers, addresses, amounts, invoice or quotation contents, the text of the error, your contacts, files, photos or location, and it carries no user or device identifier, so reports cannot be linked to you or to each other. Like any internet request, sending a report reveals your device's IP address to BugSnag, which may record it under its own policy; we never use it to identify you or link reports together. BugSnag keeps reports for a limited time and handles them under SmartBear's Privacy Policy.

Reminders. If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders (money due, quotations, backups, short business tips) on your phone itself. Nothing is sent anywhere to create them. By default a reminder on your lock screen says only, for example, "2 payments are late"; client names and amounts appear only if you turn that on in Settings → Reminders.

4. What we don't do

We don't collect, sell, rent or share your data, and we don't receive it. We don't track how you use the App. We don't show ads. We don't use third-party analytics or advertising libraries. The only third-party service the App reports to is BugSnag, for the anonymous crash reports described above.

5. Device permissions, and why

Each permission is used only for the feature named, only when you use that feature, and never to collect data for us:

Camera / Photos — to capture or choose an image for your logo, letterhead, signature or stamp.
Contacts — to import a client's name, phone number or address instead of typing it. Only the contact you pick is saved.
Fingerprint / Face ID — to unlock the App if you turn on the app lock. The check is done by your phone; the App never receives your fingerprint or face data.
Notifications — to show the reminders described above. The App asks first, and works fully without it.
Internet — only for Google Drive backup, the update check and anonymous crash reports, all described above.

The App can also lock behind a PIN you set. The PIN is never stored as-is: only a protected verifier is kept in your phone's secure key storage. Screenshots are blocked on screens that show secrets such as backup passwords.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records in the App, or remove everything with Settings → Erase all data on this phone. Uninstalling the App also removes its data from your device. Backup files you saved or shared, and backups in your Google Drive, remain until you delete them; you can delete the "BillBook Backups" folder from Google Drive at any time. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot restore it for you — keep a backup and remember its password.

7. Children's privacy

BillBook is a business tool intended for adult business owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision, published at this same address.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@billbook.store.
