Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026 · Applies to InvoiceKit v1.0.0

InvoiceKit was built to be a local-first app on purpose: your business data is yours, and it stays on your device. This policy explains, plainly and specifically, what the App stores, what it doesn't, and why it asks for the device permissions it does.

1. The short version

InvoiceKit has no account system and no backend server. There is nothing for us to collect, because nothing you enter ever leaves your device unless you personally choose to share a generated PDF through your device's own share sheet. We do not run analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind in the App.

2. What the App stores, and where

Everything below is stored in a local database on your device only, using your device's own storage — never on a server we operate, because we don't operate one:

Business profile — your business name, contact details, tax identifiers (such as GST/NTN/STRN, if you enter them), and logo/letterhead images.
Client records — names, phone numbers, and addresses you add or import from your device contacts.
Catalog — the items or services you sell, with prices and tax rates.
Documents — the invoices and quotations you create, including line items, totals, and payment records you log.
Signatures and stamps — images you capture or upload to place on documents.

3. What we don't do

We don't collect, transmit, sell, or share your data, because the App has no mechanism to do so. We don't track your usage of the App. We don't show ads. We don't use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising libraries. There is no cloud sync and no remote copy of your data anywhere.

4. Device permissions, and why

The App may ask for the following permissions, each used only for the specific feature named — never in the background, and never to collect data for us:

Camera / Photo Library — to let you capture or choose an image for your business logo or a scanned letterhead.
Contacts — to let you import a client's name, phone number, or address directly from your device's contacts instead of typing it manually.

Any permission your device's operating system lists that isn't described above (for example, one inherited from an underlying software library the App depends on) is not actively used by any InvoiceKit feature.

5. Sharing documents

When you tap "Share" on a generated invoice or quotation, the App hands that PDF to your device's native share sheet — the same system-level mechanism any app uses to share a file with WhatsApp, email, or another app you choose. That transfer is your explicit, one-time action; the App does not automatically send documents anywhere, and we have no visibility into what happens after you hand the file to another app.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. Uninstalling the App removes its local database along with it. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot restore it for you — the App's own Export/Restore feature (Settings → Export All Data / Restore Backup) is the mechanism for keeping your own backup and moving data between devices.

7. Children's privacy

InvoiceKit is a business tool intended for adult business owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and given the App's local-only design, we have no mechanism to collect data from anyone, of any age.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision, published at this same address.

9. Contact
